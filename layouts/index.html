Why Save the Lake?

Lakes play a crucial role in the water cycle, supporting biodiversity and regulating local climate. Our mission is to restore and protect Kembattahalli Lake.

Kembattahalli Lake is more than just a water body — it is the heart of our local ecosystem. Over the years, encroachment and neglect have threatened its existence. We are working tirelessly to rejuvenate the lake, restore its catchment area, and create a green belt around it.

Through community engagement, partnerships, and scientific restoration methods, we aim to bring the lake back to life — one step at a time.
