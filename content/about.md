## The Lake

Kembattahalli Lake is located in the  Kembattahalli village, Uttarahalli hobli, Bangalire South Taluk / JP Nagar 8th phase of Bengaluru, Karnataka. It spans approximately 6+ acres and serves as a crucial water recharge zone for the surrounding region. It is a vital water body in our community, supporting local biodiversity. The lake rejuvenation project is a community effort involving local residents, environmentalists, and government officials.

It all began with a few concerned residents who noticed the steady decline of Kembattahalli Lake. Encroachment, pollution, and neglect had taken a toll on what was once a thriving water body.

## Rejuvenation circa 2020

In 2019, a group of volunteers came together for a Lake conservation awareness drive in which many school children participated. With the active support of former coroporators of Gottigere and Anjanapura wards viz., Shri T.Narayana and Shri Somashekhar and MLA of Bangalore South Shri M. Krishnappa the lake which was a sewage dump was taken up for rejuvenation. The funds from the GOK under the AMRUT scheme were utilized for rejuvenation.
As with most urban lakes the Kembattahalli lake suffered/suffers from :
1. encroachment of lake area
2. encroachment of feeder channels
3. sewage water entry into the lake
4. garbage dumping in the lake premises

## Judicial Intervention - ongoing

The Hon'ble National Green Tribunal (Principal Bench, New Delhi) suo muto took up the matter of encroachment of the lake by way of a burial ground (OA 312/2021). The Tribunal ordered for the Government to allot separate burial ground elsewhere and the same has been allocated.
The other matters of lake encroachment at other places, feeder channel issues etc are being addressed via the same case now transferred to the South Zone bench of the Tribunal at Chennai (OA 132/2023)

The lake was rejuvenated and received good amount of water in the year 2022 and 2023. The lake once used as dumping ground, hub of criminal activities started being used for recreational space (walking etc)

## Today

The lake now suffers from drastic reduction of water flow since 2024. We are suspecting water has been diverted upstream but our efforts to work with govenment to set this right have not been successful so far but we are continuously woking towards it. The lake premises continue to serve as dumping ground for garbage which we are getting cleaned. 

So the primary issues are :
1. identify feeder channel network for the lake
2. ensure the feeder channel network is not encroached, not filled with garbage
3. sewage water entry into the lake (to a large extent it is reduced)
4. stop the garbage dumping in the lake premises
5. stop the lake being used as road
6. lock down all the gates to the lake atleast at night
7. remove the encroachments and fix and secure the lake perimeter


## The Vision

A rejuvenated Kembattahalli Lake that serves as a model for community-led lake conservation — proving that when people come together, they can restore even the most neglected natural treasures.


## Our Mission

To restore and maintain Kembattahalli Lake as a sustainable ecosystem that benefits both nature and the community — through scientific restoration methods, community engagement, and long-term conservation efforts.
