Our flagship project focused on the scientific rejuvenation of Kembattahalli Lake.

## What We Do

- **Weed Removal:** Clearing invasive aquatic vegetation to improve water quality.
- **Biodiversity Enhancement:** Planting native species around the lake to create a thriving ecosystem.
- **Feeder channel:**: Regular Feeder channel cleaning before and during the rainy season so that water flow to the lake is ensured.
