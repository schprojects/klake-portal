We are creating a green buffer zone around Kembattahalli Lake by planting native tree species.

## Why Trees?

Trees play a vital role in lake conservation — they prevent soil erosion, improve groundwater recharge, provide habitat for wildlife, and enhance the beauty of the area.

## Our Goal

Plant **1000+ native trees** around the lake perimeter and surrounding areas, creating a self-sustaining green belt.
