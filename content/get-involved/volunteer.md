Volunteers are the backbone of our initiative. Here's how you can contribute your time and skills.

## Ways to Volunteer

- **Clean-Up Drives:** Join our weekend clean-up events at the lake.
- **Tree Plantation:** Help us plant and maintain native trees.
- **Awareness Campaigns:** Spread the word about lake conservation.
- **Skill-Based Volunteering:** Offer your expertise in areas like legal, communication, GIS mapping, or project management.

## Sign Up

Interested? Send us an email at **welfare.kembattahalli.lake@gmail.com** with your name, area of interest, and availability. We'll get back to you with upcoming opportunities.
