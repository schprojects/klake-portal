About Klake Lake

Kembattahalli Lake is a vital water body in our community, supporting local biodiversity and serving as a source of livelihood for many. The lake rejuvenation project is a community effort involving local residents, environmentalists, and government officials.

Our Mission

To restore and maintain Klake Lake as a sustainable ecosystem that benefits both nature and the community.
